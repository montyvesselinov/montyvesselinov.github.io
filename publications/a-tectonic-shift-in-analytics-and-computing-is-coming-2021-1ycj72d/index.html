A Tectonic Shift in Analytics and Computing Is Coming

G. Morra, E. Bozdag, M. Knepley, L. Rass, V.V. Vesselinov

AGU EOS2021DOI: 10.1029/2021EO159258

Summary

This article argues that artificial intelligence combined with high-performance computing is poised to change how geoscientists extract knowledge from large and complex datasets. It highlights a broader transition toward data-driven scientific workflows capable of accelerating discovery, interpretation, and decision support across Earth and environmental sciences.
