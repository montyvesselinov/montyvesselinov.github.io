			<p>My primary research focus is on developing unsupervised (self-supervised) and physics-informed ML methods.</p>
			<p>The methods utilize Matrix/Tensor Decomposition (Factorization) coupled with physics, sparsity and nonnegativity constraints. The methods are capable to reveal the temporal and spatial footprints of the extracted features.</p>
			<h4>SmartTensors</h4>
			<p><a href="https://SmartTensors.com" target="_blank">SmartTensors</a> is a general framework for Unsupervised and Physics-Informed Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence (ML/AI).</p>
			<p><a href="https://SmartTensors.com" target="_blank">SmartTensors</a> incorporates a novel unsupervised ML based on tensor decomposition coupled with physics, sparsity and nonnegativity constraints.</p>
			<p><a href="https://SmartTensors.com" target="_blank">SmartTensors</a> has been applied to extract the temporal and spatial footprints of the features in multi-dimensional datasets in the form of multi-way arrays or tensors.</p>
			<p><a href="https://SmartTensors.com" target="_blank">SmartTensors</a> algorithms called <strong>NMFk</strong> and <strong>NTFk</strong> for Matrix/Tensor Factorization (Decomposition) coupled with sparsity and nonnegativity constraints custom k-means clustering has been developed in <a href="http://julialang.org">Julia</a></p>
			<p><a href="https://SmartTensors.com" target="_blank">SmartTensors</a> software packages are available as open source on <a href="https://github.com/orgs/SmartTensors">GitHub</a>.</p>

				<p> Other key methods/tools for ML include: </p>
				<ul>
					<li> <a href="https://github.com/madsjulia/RegAE.jl" target="_blank">RegAE.jl</a>: a novel curring-edge methodology for performing inverse analysis. The main goal of this approach is to provide an efficient and general means of regularizing inverse problems where the parameter fields are high-dimensional and have coherent structures. <a href="https://github.com/madsjulia/RegAE.jl" target="_blank">RegAE.jl</a> is intended to work for any general inverse problem without prior knowledge of the solved physics and how to properly perform the regularization. <a href="https://github.com/madsjulia/RegAE.jl" target="_blank">RegAE.jl</a> leverages a Variational AutoEncoder (VAE) to learn how to regularize these problems based on unconditioned realizations of the high-dimensional parameter fields. <a href="https://github.com/madsjulia/RegAE.jl" target="_blank">RegAE.jl</a> provides a computationally efficient means of obtaining an optimal solution by (1) easing the
						regularization process, and (2) reducing the dimensionality of the optimized parameter space. In addition, the application of adjoint methods (via automatic differentiation) during the <a href="https://github.com/madsjulia/RegAE.jl" target="_blank">RegAE.jl</a> inverse analysis further reduces the computational cost. </li>
					<li> <a href="https://github.com/madsjulia/SVR.jl" target="_blank">SVR.jl</a>: Support Vector Regression (SVR) analysis. </li>

	<li>Model Diagnostics
		<div>
			<p> Model diagnostics work executed under most of the projects and practical applications has been performed using the wide range of novel theoretical methods and computational tools developed over the years. </p>
			<p> A key tool for model diagnostics is the <strong>MADS</strong> (Model Analysis &amp; Decision Support) framework. <strong>MADS</strong> is an integrated open-source high-performance computational (HPC) framework. </p>
			<div class="legacy-plot">
				<img src="logos/mads_white_swan_logo_big_text.png" width="40%" alt="">
			</div>
			<p> <strong>MADS</strong> can execute a wide range of data- and model-based analyses: </p>
			<ul>
				<li> Sensitivity Analysis </li>
				<li> Parameter Estimation (PE), Model Inversion and Calibration </li>
				<li> Uncertainty Quantification (UQ) </li>
				<li> Model Selection and Model Averaging </li>
				<li> Model Reduction and Surrogate Modeling </li>
				<li> Machine Learning and Blind Source Separation </li>
				<li> Decision Analysis and Support </li>
			</ul>
			<p> <strong>MADS</strong> has been tested to perform HPC simulations on a wide-range multi-processor clusters and parallel environments (Moab, Slurm, etc.). </p>
			<p> <strong>MADS</strong> utilizes adaptive rules and techniques which allows the analyses to be performed with a minimum user input. </p>
			<p> <strong>MADS</strong> provides a series of alternative algorithms to execute each type of data- and model-based analyses. </p>
			<p> <strong>MADS</strong> can be externally coupled with any existing simulator through integrated modules that generate input files required by the simulator and parse output files generated by the simulator using a set of template and instruction files. </p>
			<p> <strong>MADS</strong> also provides internally coupling with a series of built-in analytical simulators of groundwater flow and contaminant transport in aquifers. </p>
			<p> <strong>MADS</strong> has been successfully applied to perform various model analyses related to environmental management of contamination sites. Examples include solutions of source identification problems, quantification of uncertainty, model calibration, and optimization of monitoring networks. </p>
			<p> <strong>MADS</strong> current stable version has been actively updated. </p>
			<p> <strong>MADS</strong> software, source code, and example input/output files are available at the <a href="http://madsjulia.github.io" target="_blank">MADS website</a>. </p>
